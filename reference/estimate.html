How we sized the plant & priced each unit

Both are worked out from Safal Industries’ own MSEDCL bill — nothing assumed.

1 · System size → 50 kWp

Solar produces only in the 09:00–17:00 window (Zone C) — ~4,633 units/month, about 81% of your daily use; the 06:00–09:00 slot (Zone B, ~36 units) is negligible and is excluded. Your 12-month daytime load needs ~46 kWp for full cover; this system is sized at 50 kWp to fully meet daytime demand with headroom for load growth (a small daytime surplus exports).

2 · Per-unit value → ₹6.56/unit

Built bottom-up from your tariff: base energy ₹7.66 + demand ₹1.52 + FAC ₹0.20 + tax-on-sale ₹0.29 + daytime ToD rebate −₹1.15 − Grid Support Charge ₹1.96 = ₹6.56/unit — the real value each solar unit offsets, net of the ToD rebate and the GSC.
